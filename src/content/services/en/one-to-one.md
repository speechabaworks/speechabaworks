Most people arrive with one of four things: a limited vocal learner who has plateaued, an AAC system that is being abandoned, a behavior plan that is fighting the communication plan, or a team that cannot agree.

Sessions are working sessions. I review your data, your video and your draft goals, and each session ends with a decision rather than a list of things to consider.

### On international rates

I consult with families and clinicians across the United States, the United Kingdom, Australia, mainland China, Taiwan and Hong Kong. A rate that is reasonable in New York is not reasonable everywhere, and I would rather adjust the rate than lose the conversation. Ask.
