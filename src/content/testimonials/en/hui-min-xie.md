  As the mother of an autistic child, and as a frontline intervention teacher, I have felt Ms Linda Chow's professional expertise and warm support very deeply.

  Professionally, Ms Linda has visited our center many times and brought us exceptionally valuable guidance. She laid out a clear intervention sequence for speech sound development and gave us a scientific, workable teaching framework. Under her guidance, many children at our center who previously could not produce sounds, or whose speech was unclear, have gradually developed clear verbal expression and gone on to communicate independently. This has not only changed the children's lives and improved their quality of life, but also rekindled hope for countless families.

  In my role as a mother, the augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) system Ms Linda recommended brought a transformative change for my child, JinBao. Since we introduced it, JinBao's problem behaviors have decreased significantly and his speech has become clearer. He is finally able to express more of his needs and thoughts. Even more remarkably, since he began using AAC, he can hold simple everyday conversations with us in areas we never explicitly taught, and he has become more confident and cheerful overall.

  What I admire most about Ms Linda is that she always pinpoints each child's unique needs and provides individualized support. Whether working with a toddler with delayed language development or a case with severe communication disorders, she draws on deep professional knowledge to help them make real progress in communication.

  She is not only an outstanding expert in language and behavior intervention, but also an empathetic guide. Her professional guidance has both improved my teaching and completely changed my child's developmental trajectory. I am sincerely grateful to Ms Linda, and I strongly recommend her services to every family and practitioner who needs help.

  而在我作为母亲的角色中，Linda老师推荐的扩大沟通系统（AAC），更是为我的孩子金宝带来了翻天覆地的变化。自从引入这套系统后，金宝的问题行为显著减少，发音也变得更加清晰，他终于能够更多地表达自己的需求和想法，更神奇的是自从使用了扩大沟通系统，在没有教导的对话领域，孩子也能够简单的和我们进行日常生活中常用的对话，整个人也变得更加自信和开朗。

  Linda老师最令人敬佩的，是她总能精准地捕捉到每个孩子的独特需求，并提供个性化的支持。无论是面对语言发展迟缓的幼儿，还是沟通障碍严重的个案，她都能凭借深厚的专业功底，帮助他们在沟通能力上取得切实的进步。

  她不仅是一位卓越的语言与行为干预专家，更是一位充满同理心的引路人。她的专业指导，既提升了我的教学能力，也彻底改变了我孩子的成长轨迹。我由衷地感谢Linda老师，也强烈推荐她的专业服务给每一位需要帮助的家庭和从业者。
