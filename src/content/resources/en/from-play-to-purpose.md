Phase 1 establishes *you* as the source of good things. By Phase 6 there is structured learning. In between are four phases most programs skip, which is usually why the structured learning does not hold.

Three rules govern the whole sequence: advance only after three consecutive sessions with minimal disruptive behavior, never use physical force, and always build engagement from the learner's own interests.
