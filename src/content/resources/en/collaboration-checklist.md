Four sections — establishing values and ethics, clarifying roles and competencies, communication and teamwork, and integrated action — with a translation table for the terms each profession uses for the same thing ("communicative intent" and "verbal operant" being the classic pair).

Designed to be opened during a case review, not read beforehand.
